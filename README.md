## About Me

I am an **accountant based in Manila, Philippines**, developing my knowledge and practical skills in Australian bookkeeping and Xero.

My current focus includes bank reconciliation, accounts payable and receivable, financial reporting, Australian GST fundamentals, payroll processes, and month-end bookkeeping.

I am building practical portfolio projects using fictional business data while working toward **Xero certification**. I am interested in remote junior bookkeeping opportunities where I can support Australian businesses and work under appropriate professional supervision.
